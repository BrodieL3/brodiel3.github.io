Privacy

This site doesn't run its own trackers, and it doesn't ask you to fill out anything directly on gurfc.com. Here's the handful of places visitor data touches a third party.

What this site does

gurfc.com is a static page with no analytics, no advertising pixels, no cookies of its own, and no server collecting or storing anything you do here.

Third-party content on this page

  • Instagram posts in the gallery are embedded directly from Instagram. Loading them lets Meta see that request and, depending on your browser settings, set its own cookies — the same as visiting instagram.com directly. See Instagram's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts load from Google's servers for the site's typefaces, which sends your browser's IP address to Google. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Alumni Newsletter signup links out to a Google Form. Anything you submit there is handled by Google and by GURFC's club officers who read the responses, not by this website.
  • Text/Email the Captain buttons open your own phone or email app — nothing is sent through this site.
